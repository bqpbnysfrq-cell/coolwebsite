Webdesign und
Leitsysteme
Crossover is a DnB music
festival based in Austria. The design
process was envisioned by visualizing
the physical and digital presence of the
event, thus manifesting an aesthetic that
maintains high-contrast values
and retro-futuristic visuals.
Since the festival takes place mainly
outdoors, a Leitsystem with nighttime
visibility was taken into consideration.
